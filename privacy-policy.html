Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 24, 2026

Grounded Messaging ("the App") is operated by Grounded Pursuit. This policy explains what the App sends, what it keeps, and what it never touches.

1. What the App Sends

The App has an AI practice mode. When it is on, the text you type — your practice reply, the scenario you are practising, and any message you paste into New Simulation or Self Decode — is sent to a server we operate (a Google Apps Script endpoint), which forwards it to Google's Gemini API to generate the feedback and the other person's reply.

Sent with each request: the message text, the scenario context, and your practice settings (who you are practising with, pursuer or withdrawer).

Never sent: your name, email address, contacts, location, phone number, device identifiers, or any account information. The App has no accounts and never asks for any of that.

If you paste a real message from a real person, that text is sent. We recommend removing names and identifying details before pasting anything private.

2. What We Store

We do not run a database and we do not keep your conversations. Our proxy passes each request straight through and keeps only one number: a daily count of how many AI requests it has served in total, used to cap spending. That counter contains no message text and nothing that identifies you.

Google's own infrastructure logs requests as part of running the Apps Script and Gemini services. Those logs are subject to Google's Privacy Policy and the Gemini API terms, not ours.

3. Offline Mode

The App also has a built-in offline grader. When AI mode is off, when you have used your daily AI turns, or when the network is unavailable, the App grades your reply entirely on your device and nothing is sent anywhere. The footer of the App always shows which mode you are in.

4. On-Device Storage

The App uses your browser's local storage to remember your progress, your settings, and your daily AI usage count. That data stays on your device, is never transmitted to us, and is cleared if you clear the App's data.

5. Third-Party Services

The App loads no third-party code. Its styling and icons are built into the page itself, so nothing is fetched from an outside CDN and no other company sees your IP address when you open it. The only server the App contacts is our own proxy, and only when AI mode is on, as described in section 1.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for adults (18+). We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information through the App, please contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
